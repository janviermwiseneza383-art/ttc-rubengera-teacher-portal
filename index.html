Assessment Guide

Calculation rules used by the portal

Percentage
(Total Obtained ÷ Total Maximum) × 100
Scaled Score
(Percentage ÷ 100) × Target Scale
Grade Scale
A: 80–100%
B: 70–79.9%
C: 60–69.9%
D: 50–59.9%
F: Below 50%

Assessment Tips

  • Enter a meaningful assessment name.
  • Maximum marks must be greater than zero.
  • Obtained marks cannot exceed maximum marks.
  • Use the add-test button for extra assessments.
  • The total is calculated automatically.
